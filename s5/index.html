01

Where Things Stand

The format is locked. The teams are not. Barry's S5 proposal — four four-man teams, ten weeks, all four classes, with R5 and R10 running all four on track at once like S4 — passed its poll 14–0. Settings carry over from S4 except a flat 10-minute qualifier. Registration posts Thursday July 30 at noon ET, and the first 16 to sign up claim guaranteed spots.

What's open is how the teams get built. This page ranks the captain candidates using every tournament Hairpin has ever run — all 17 in the tournament app plus the two Wednesday Gr.3 seasons that only exist on this site. That's four Multi-Class seasons, not one, and it changes the answer.

The Three Proposals

From #multi-class-s5-chat, July 29.

Boltus
Time-Trial Shootout

Everyone drives the same car, the fastest four become captains, then they pick. Backed by esully: "Not a bad plan IMO."

Risk Rewards one hot lap, not ten weeks of showing up. t0pher is a Super Formula champion on 8 of 23 rounds started this year — exactly the profile a time trial promotes and attendance should demote.
iCrazy
Top or Bottom 4, Reverse Picks

Four captains from last season's top four or bottom four. Picks run in reverse order — S4's P1 picks 4th, P2 picks 3rd. Draft day on a call or streamed.

Right mechanism Reverse-order picking is the correct balancing tool and should be kept. The "bottom 4" half doesn't survive contact with the data — see section 07.
Barry
Past Standings

"I was also leaning on using past standings depending on who signs up." Captains seeded off prior results once the 16-driver field is known.

Right instinct, wrong window One season is a 10-race sample. Across four Multi-Class seasons and 15 other tournaments the order shuffles noticeably — and produces materially fairer teams.

How These Are Ranked

A captain here does three jobs: drive fast, show up all ten weeks, and coordinate four drivers across four classes every race night. The Captain Index weighs all three. Older results still count but are discounted — a 2025 season carries about a third of the weight of a live one.

Three live objections, all fair

Barry is the admin. Captaining while running registration, the schedule and the draft is real work and invites awkward optics. If he'd rather stay neutral, swap in iCrazy — second-best Multi-Class record ever, two career titles, and he proposed the draft. Team spread only moves 3.9% → 4.7%.

Barry and Jett are slipping in this specific format (P8 and P9 in S4). If the room wants captains picked purely on Multi-Class pace, the honest answer is Sully, iCrazy, Dark Delacroix and TVDV — but accept the 8.6% spread and iCrazy's 19-of-34 attendance that come with it.

Speedysully has the best live form of anyone outside the four. He now leads Group 1 on 133 points after winning Interlagos on July 27, with a perfect 17-of-17 attendance record in 2026 and the steepest improvement curve in the league — P14 in Multi-Class S3 to P6 in S4. Two Multi-Class seasons is a thin record to captain on, but he is the first name to add if the field is bigger than 16 or anyone above him passes.

Three things to settle before Thursday noon

  1. Drop the "bottom 4" option. It reads fair and isn't. CtillVR is running second in Group 1 right now and TheEliteWeasel61 is the lowest-ranked four-season driver in the league — the set treats them as equivalent, and hands the weekly four-driver coordination job to the drivers with the least of that experience.
  2. Make the time trial a tiebreak, not the method. Use it to set pick order among agreed captains. As a selection filter it promotes exactly the wrong profile — t0pher is a Super Formula champion on 8 of 23 rounds, and has never finished a Multi-Class season.
  3. Publish an attendance expectation with the registration post. This format punishes absence four times harder than S4 did: one missing driver is 25% of a team's score, and R5 and R10 run all four classes at once. A one-line "if you can't make 8 of 10, take a reserve spot" would do it.
